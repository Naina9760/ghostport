# Code of conduct

GhostPort contributors and maintainers must participate respectfully and
professionally. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project discussions should stay focused on technical evidence, user safety, and
constructive criticism. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner, `Naina9760`, through
an established channel. Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue.
